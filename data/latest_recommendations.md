# How to reduce cart abandonment

*2026-11-06 16:45* · **You asked:** How can I reduce cart abandonment

Here are 3 recommendations to reduce cart abandonment:

1. **Target high mobile abandonment rate** (effort: Medium)  
   - **Do this:** Explore ways to optimize the mobile checkout experience, such as improving page load speed and simplifying forms.  
   - **Evidence (2016-17):** mobile: 85.8% abandoned, +14.4 points vs. overall, 4,748 sessions; $171,885.43 abandoned  
   - **Why it may help:** Mobile users have a higher abandonment rate and lower checkout completion.  
   - **How to test it:** Monitor mobile abandonment rates and checkout completions after implementing changes.

2. **Address first-time visitor issues** (effort: Medium)  
   - **Do this:** Consider using pop-ups or reminders guiding first-time visitors through the checkout process.  
   - **Evidence (2016-17):** first visit: 78.2% abandoned, +6.7 points vs. overall, 17,149 sessions; $619,614.52 abandoned  
   - **Why it may help:** First-time visitors abandon at a higher rate, possibly due to uncertainty or navigation issues.  
   - **How to test it:** A/B test different approaches with first-time visitors and track their checkout rates.

3. **Increase engagement for returning customers** (effort: Medium)  
   - **Do this:** Implement strategies to encourage repeat purchases from past customers, such as personalized recommendations or follow-up emails after previous purchases.  
   - **Evidence (2016-17):** bought before: 45.0% abandoned, -26.5 points vs. overall, 2,275 sessions; $77,403.18 abandoned  
   - **Why it may help:** Returning customers have a lower abandonment rate and complete more purchases overall.  
   - **How to test it:** Measure repeat purchase rates and historical cart recovery success for returning customers after implementation.

- Focus on optimizing the mobile experience and enhancing support for first-time visitors.
- Evidence figures are computed in code. They show patterns in Google Merchandise Store data, not causes, and not Jb Prints shoppers.
- The ideas are the AI's. None has been tested, so no improvement is promised.
