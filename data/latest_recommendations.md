# How to reduce cart abandonment

*2026-11-19 16:40* · **You asked:** How can I reduce cart abandonment?

Here are 4 recommendations to reduce cart abandonment:

1. **Reduce mobile abandonment** (effort: Medium)  
   - **Do this:** Improve mobile checkout experience and simplification to reduce abandonment rates.  
   - **Evidence (2016-17):** mobile: 85.8% abandoned, +14.4 points vs. overall, 4,748 sessions; $171,885.43 abandoned  
   - **Why it may help:** Mobile has the highest abandonment rate (85.8%) among device types, indicating difficulty in the mobile checkout process.  
   - **How to test it:** Measure mobile checkout completion rates before and after changes.

2. **Target first-time buyers** (effort: High)  
   - **Do this:** Enhance engagement for first-time buyers with optimized onboarding messages or tutorials during checkout.  
   - **Evidence (2016-17):** never bought: 73.4% abandoned, +1.9 points vs. overall, 31,431 sessions; $1,147,730.56 abandoned  
   - **Why it may help:** First-time buyers have an abandonment rate of 73.4%, suggesting they may need additional support to complete their purchases.  
   - **How to test it:** Track first-time buyer conversion rates pre- and post-implementation for changes.

3. **Optimize checkout stage reminders** (effort: Medium)  
   - **Do this:** Focus on tailored reminders for carts left at checkout to address common checkout issues.  
   - **Evidence (2016-17):** reached checkout: 35.0% abandoned, -36.4 points vs. overall, 14,798 sessions; $268,154.73 abandoned  
   - **Why it may help:** The abandonment rate for those who reach checkout is lower (35.0%), indicating a targeted reminder could be effective.  
   - **How to test it:** Monitor the influence of reminders on conversion rates for carts left at checkout before and after changes.

4. **Enhance first and repeat visit strategies** (effort: Low)  
   - **Do this:** Implement strategies to convert first-time visits with immediate follow-up messaging after cart abandonment.  
   - **Evidence (2016-17):** first visit: 78.2% abandoned, +6.7 points vs. overall, 17,149 sessions; $619,614.52 abandoned  
   - **Why it may help:** First-time visits account for 78.2% of abandonment which could be reduced with quick engagement.  
   - **How to test it:** Analyze conversion rates from first-time visitors pre- and post-engagement efforts for abandoned carts.

- Evidence figures are computed in code. They show patterns in Google Merchandise Store data, not causes.
- The ideas are the AI's. None has been tested, so no improvement is promised.
