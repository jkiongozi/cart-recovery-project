# Jb Prints store policies

## Discounts
- Maximum recovery discount allowed: 5%
- The 5% discount is only for returning customers (customers who have bought from the store before)
- Welcome offer: first-time buyers automatically get 10% off when they subscribe to the shop's emails
- The 10% welcome offer and the 5% discount can never be combined
- First-time buyers must not be offered the 5% discount
- Discounts allowed on sale items: no

## Shipping
- Free shipping threshold: none (customers pay shipping)
- Free shipping allowed as a recovery incentive: yes
- Average shipping cost per order: $7.00 (this is what free shipping costs the store per recovered order)

## Costs
- Average product cost as % of price: 60% (the store keeps 40% of each product's price; prices differ by product, so cost = 60% of that product's price)
- Cost to send one recovery email: $0.00 (Shopify email)
